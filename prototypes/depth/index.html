About this preview

This explores a DepthFlow-style effect for the atlas’s fullscreen viewer. A depth map is prepared once; your browser draws the moving perspective live. There is no video download or AI model running on your device in this demo.

Drag with a mouse or one finger, then release to hold that viewpoint. Adjust 3D depth from flat to 3×, or try the presets. Slow drift explores the photo automatically; dragging takes over at any time.

Depth is an estimate. Stronger settings crop in further and can stretch edges, thin structures and reflections. Hold for original to compare the untouched photograph.

On the atlas, this would be an optional fullscreen control. This standalone preview has no connection to your private photo library. Integration awaits your review.
