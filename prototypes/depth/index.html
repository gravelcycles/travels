About this preview

This explores a DepthFlow-style effect for the atlas’s fullscreen viewer. A depth map is prepared once; your browser draws the moving perspective live. There is no video download or AI model running on your device in this demo.

Drag with a mouse or one finger, then release to hold that viewpoint. Choose Mouse move to follow your pointer, or Slow drift to move automatically. Movement stays gentle; dragging takes control at any time.

Depth is an estimate. A small crop leaves room to move; edges, thin structures and reflections can still distort. Hold for original to compare the untouched photograph.

On the atlas, this would be an optional fullscreen control. Integration into the atlas viewer awaits your review.
