DAY 6 · 18 Aug

Brienz, Interlaken & Mürren

Mürren · day trip

This was the grand connection day: train to Brienz, boat across the lake to Interlaken, train into Lauterbrunnen, gondola to Grütschalp, and the little mountain railway to Mürren. The return retraced the high section before continuing by rail from Interlaken to Luzern, a full loop made from nine ordered legs.
